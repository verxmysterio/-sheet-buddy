# SHEET BUDDY — DATA PRIVACY POLICY & ACCOUNTANT CLIENT CONFIDENTIALITY

At **Sheet Buddy**, we recognize that Chartered Accountants, tax practitioners, business owners, and accounting professionals handle sensitive financial data. Our systems are architected with privacy-by-design principles.

---

## 1. Zero PDF Storage Policy

1. **No Statement File Retention**: When you upload a bank statement PDF, it resides temporarily in volatile RAM for parsing and is never saved to the server's permanent disk storage.
2. **Instant Purge**: Upon completion of the parsing request, all binary document streams are closed and discarded from active memory.
3. **Structured Data Discretion**: Only structured transaction summaries (dates, narrations, amounts, and XML export strings) requested for your Tally export are retained in your encrypted user account database so you can re-download them from your history archive.

---

## 2. Client & Multi-Client Confidentiality

- **Tenant Isolation**: Statements processed under one account or client profile are strictly isolated and never accessible or visible to other users.
- **No Third-Party Data Sharing**: Sheet Buddy does not sell, lease, or distribute financial transactions, customer statements, or accounting ledger names to third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing entities.
- **Client Tagging**: CA firms can tag transactions with arbitrary internal client IDs or PAN numbers without exposing data outside their workspace.

---

## 3. Communication & Support Privacy

- **Support Tickets**: Support requests submitted via the platform or WhatsApp are only accessed by authorized personnel for troubleshooting and resolving user queries.
- **Payment Verification**: Payment receipts sent via WhatsApp are used solely to verify UPI transactions and credit the corresponding user account.
